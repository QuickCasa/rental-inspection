For landlords and tenants

Free move-in and move-out inspection app

Walk through a rental on your phone, room by room. Rate each item, add notes and photos, collect signatures and download a PDF report that shows when every photo was added and every signature was made. It works offline, there's no account, and nothing leaves your device.

Inspections on this device

    None yet. Inspections you start are saved in this browser.

    A backup file holds one inspection and its photos. Use it to move an inspection to another device, or to keep it safe.

    To use it like an app, add this page to your home screen. Once it has loaded, it works without a connection.

    How the move-out comparison works

    Start the move-out from the move-in inspection, and every item shows its move-in rating and notes next to the new ones. The report lists everything rated worse than at move-in, so the landlord and the tenant talk about the deposit from the same record.

    Keep your records

    Inspections are stored in this browser on this device, and nowhere else. Clearing your browsing data deletes them. For every inspection you need to keep, download the PDF and save a backup file, and send the signed PDF to everyone who signed it.

    Before you rely on it

    Some provinces and states have their own inspection form, or rules about when an inspection happens and who has to be there. Check your local rules. The report records a rental's condition. It isn't legal advice.

    Why it's free

    QuickCasa built this for landlords and renters and released it as free software under the GNU Affero General Public License. Anyone can read the code to check that photos stay on the device, and anyone who runs a changed copy for other people has to share their changes.
